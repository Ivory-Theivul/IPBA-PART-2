  • Safety & Sustainability Compliance

    What is it?

    Safety and sustainability compliance refers to the act of following workshop rules, technical standards and carrying responsible practice guidelines that protect not only individual users, but the industrial materials and/or machines through proper machine handling, protective gear, hazard awareness and safer procedures. Caring for materials, energy and the environment encourages minimizing waste, reusing resources, managing scraps, and choosing efficient methods so projects are made safely and with respect for both the individuals around, and the environment aswell.

Who is affected?

Students, Instructors, Factory Workers, Engineers, and Employees

  • Lack of awareness in safety practices.
  • Outdated equipment & poor maintenancea
  • Weak oversight & lack of accountability
  • weak and outdated systems

Relevance and Importance⋆.˚

why is it relevant and important?

It's the foundation of every IA practice. It keeps people safe and free from injury while working with tools, machinery and different materials. To add to this, it teaches you to value and learn to conserve limited resources and reduce waste, building habits that help the environment and skills that prepare you for careers and a lifelong responsibility where safety and care for resources are essential for good work and professional standards.
